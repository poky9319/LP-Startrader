RULES & FAQ

How ranking works

Where does UID come from?

UID is the STARTRADER live account number. The broker sends a daily file with UID and ROI. We do not invent IDs — if your UID is missing, you are not on that day's report yet.

Where does the name come from?

The public name is the display name you send on Telegram when you register for the cup. Use a nickname (e.g. Alex T.). We do not publish your legal name from KYC.

How is ROI calculated?

ROI is taken from STARTRADER's daily competition export. We publish the figure they provide, ranked from high to low. The same method is used for every participant.

Who can join?

New or existing clients who open / link a live account via the FXUlti IB link, deposit at least $50, and submit UID + nickname before or during the month.

When does the month reset?

A new season starts on the 1st of each month. The previous month's final board is frozen after the last 08:00 update.

Is this an official STARTRADER page?

No. This is an FXUlti IB competition traded on STARTRADER accounts. STARTRADER provides the daily data; FXUlti publishes this independent leaderboard.
